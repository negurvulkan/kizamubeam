# KizamuBeam manual

KizamuBeam takes you from the first idea to the finished piece in one app: draw and write directly on the workspace, turn photos into engravings, generate boxes, gears and slot-together models, check everything in 3D and send the job straight to your laser. It is free software (GPL-3.0), needs no account and runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.
